# Foundational pulse and reaction screening ✨

These early calculations identify physical constraints and rejected configurations that remain useful when exploring a new pulse design. Their numerical outputs use older simplified models and are not the selected plant's predictions. Use the [current pulse methods](methods.md) and [nuclear review](nuclear-review.md) for the selected network, rates and updated calculations. The original detailed notes and broader historical record are in the [v0.1 research log](https://github.com/tkwa/protium-fusion/blob/v0.1/docs/protium-fusion/RESEARCH-LOG.md).

- The steady-state screen in [nuclear_screen.py](../nuclear_screen.py) follows the CN-I and hot-CNO cycle and its beta-decay waiting times. The [staged-capture screen](../staged_capture_screen.py) explores moving slow decays outside the hot pulse; the associated waiting time and catalyst inventory still need to be counted.
- In the homogeneous expanding-sphere [pulse model](../pulse_model.py), a combined N15-to-O14 stage at 300 MK, 10⁸ kg/m³ and 1 km self-heated to about 1.55 GK and left only roughly 8% of its catalyst in the intended O14 output, with much of the remainder in O16. This rejects that specific two-stage parameter choice, not all combined-stage designs. It motivated separating helium production and carbon return. Reaction heat and isotope losses need to be included when optimizing temperature and composition.
- Alpha-induced leakage and hot-cycle branches rise with temperature; coefficients alone do not determine losses without isotope abundances. The more complete 27-isotope network, updated evaluations and rate sensitivities are described in the [nuclear review](nuclear-review.md).
- [Two-proton reaction screening](../two_proton_screen.py) examines O15(2p,γ)Ne17 and Ne18(2p,γ)Mg20 with explicit rate envelopes. Its constant-density and abundance assumptions make it a sensitivity screen, not a replacement for a coupled reaction network.
- Reprocessing leaked oxygen or manufacturing carbon from helium was explored as an auxiliary route. Those early calculations exposed additional pulse, recycling and energy costs. The current external-carbon cycle and its separate inventory are documented in the [current mixture selection](README.md) and [component design](../component-design/README.md).
- The prescribed converging-flow model improves on an initially compressed sphere by including the inflow energy, but it does not establish how to prepare the flow. [Driver physics](../launcher-reference/driver-physics/README.md) preserves the cold-contact and numerical-failure studies needed for further work.

The initial screens use their own temperatures, densities, reaction libraries and opacity approximations. Numerical convergence, material EOS, radiation transport, mixing and driver efficiency remain separate from their bookkeeping checks.
