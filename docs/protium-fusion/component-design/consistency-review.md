## What the source work establishes

- Electrostatic/RF repeated-gap acceleration, alternating-gradient transport and neutralization have an explicit order, energy debit, current and aperture scale. The envelope is a mathematical transport example; the peak factor, finite electrodes, beam loading and longitudinal phase control remain assumptions (`launcher.md:110–122,165–180,239`).
- FPEOS/FEOS provide nonideal contact thermodynamic scales. The entropy map depends on the chosen parallel-sector geometry, shock density and local kinetic energy (`launcher.md:128,193–195`). It supplies an initial radial temperature profile and a compensating radial-velocity change at fixed prepared energy.
- Full-network **spherical hot-flow** runs show that the tested A/C13 mapped profiles can burn, and the material/plant integration uses their actual isotope outputs (`variant_source64.md:21–29`). They do not simulate grain first contact, spatial filling, lateral shocks, vorticity, channel mixing or the central seed.
- The apparent N≈2000 success is neither a count of physical accelerators nor a derived minimum number of angular directions. One region can receive many columns, and engineered fanning or a different contact density changes its entropy. More uniform delivery can cost more hardware than the additional yield is worth.
- The cold capsules themselves remain conditional. A carbon pressure skin lowers a methane capsule's total H:C below four; a different skin introduces different material/EOS/isotope inputs. `launcher.md:215,235–237` already calls this out. Neither a warm-dense entropy calculation nor a whole-grain absorbed-energy budget proves intact acceleration, cold flight or controlled breakup.

## Smallest useful next assembly calculation

Begin with the newly important **B1 H2/H1.5 entropy response**, preserving its lower-density reference and actual physical duration. The parent has now found that a uniform +20% initial-temperature change at fixed total energy can sharply reduce B1 gain, while −20% remains productive. This is a sensitivity of particular profiles, not a universal 20% tolerance. A B1-specific contact map and a constructive controlled-collision profile are the immediate smaller discriminator before a large 3D job.

Then model one representative **three-dimensional contact patch** with a small group of neighbouring finite streams and their actual empty gaps. Generate upstream positions, velocities, chirp and mass flux from a finite-outlet/packet design; do not initialize a full solid-density spherical shell and call it assembly. Use the selected capsule composition and a nonideal EOS. Ballistic grains can be advanced until contact, with resolved continuum regions after shocks/ionization where that description is justified. A local patch should include a junction between at least three directions, since a two-stream planar collision does not represent the lateral flow at junctions.

The first pass can omit burning while temperatures and residence times make it negligible. Record conservation, entropy by mass, radial momentum, transverse kinetic stress, density nonuniformity, mixing, temporal spread and the first location where nuclear reactions become consequential. Vary one or two realistic geometric choices and refine the patch enough to distinguish physical mixing from numerical viscosity. Feed the resulting radial distribution and its angular dispersion into the existing burn/whole-cycle ledger; retain hotspots rather than replacing every parcel by its mean temperature. An ensuing global/sector reactive calculation is warranted if lateral flows or seed coupling cannot be bounded from that patch.

Success is a useful whole-plant trade between delivered energy, reusable isotope flow and actual source/receiver equipment. No universal 95% yield, 9 nrad pointing tolerance, 4 µs timing gate or fixed angular count is imposed. The separate fast seed should then be tested against the selected main inflow's pressure and density, using its already-budgeted energy. A physical experiment is ultimately needed for capsule charging/neutralization and collision/fragmentation under the selected material conditions; an early scaled experiment should target that phase-space/entropy transition rather than claim reactor-scale fusion gain.
