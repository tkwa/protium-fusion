## What the second phase resolved

Hot-core opacity has ample margins on the saved trajectories, including the smaller pulse. The distinction between true absorption and scattering is explicitly checked. Cold contact thermodynamics and shock handling remain consequential. Coarse cold-inflow calculations cannot supply an observed derating: finer runs reach high density and hit a temperature guard with strongly different partial yields. The article's2.6 is therefore a fictional outcome selected below the completed favorable small-hot reference.

The ion calculation resolves a missing loss mechanism in the specified linear-response geometry: radial harmonics absorb energy even for waves faster than parallel ion motion. It does not exclude all nonlinear or chirped extraction designs. Whole-inventory reset costs can be computed without claiming selective clearing; short resets do not make the reference power plant viable, while optimistic longer cycles retain a separate dependence on continuous isotope extraction.

Channel coating composition and thickness affect neutron availability. A cold radiator's emitting area alone omits heat routing. These checks inform the engineering account without selecting an unverified complete plant.
