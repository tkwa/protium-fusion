## Acceptance criteria

- Ordinary hydrogen is the net nuclear fuel and helium is the principal product. Account for catalyst consumption, leakage and regeneration. Consuming boron, deuterium or manufactured neutrons is a different fuel cycle unless their production is included.
- Seek a complete system below one Jupiter mass, with at least 0.001 W/kg of net useful output. Count active fuel, confinement, ballast, fields, conversion, cooling, maintenance and required fuel-processing inventories. Report gross nuclear heat separately.
- Compare steady opaque plasmas, pulsed or staged catalytic burning, particle beams and compact-gravity-assisted arrangements. A compact gravitating seed is a conditional branch whose manufacture, mass and other energy sources must be explicit.
- Account for radiation transport, pressure, instabilities, composition evolution, residence time, particle stopping, recirculating power and startup energy. Optical depth and a reaction rate alone do not prove viability.
- Investigate economics and chronology after establishing the engineering assumptions. Existing canon excludes practical protium fusion in the early 2040s; the current reassessment selects first net generation in 2049.
- User authorized investigation up to ten million tokens; this is a ceiling, not a target expenditure. Do not add precision or declare a solution merely to exhaust the budget.

## Evidence levels

1. Measured nuclear data and established physical laws.
2. Published calculations with stated assumptions.
3. Reproducible calculations in this investigation.
4. Engineering hypotheses requiring further analysis.
5. Selected future history, recorded in the current reassessment decisions and kept distinct from real evidence.

## Initial research tracks

1. Temperature/density/composition sweep using a named, versioned nuclear rate library; compare closed-cycle throughput with initial transient captures and catalyst leakage.
2. Radiation transport and pressure: optically thin cooling, absorption/scattering, Rosseland diffusion, LTE validity and boundary conditions.
3. Confinement: material and magnetic stresses, inertial/flow costs and compact gravity; include whole-system mass.
4. Beam routes: measured capture resonances versus stopping, scattering and phase-space recovery; separate neutral targets from nonneutral beams.
5. Net energy and economics: startup amortization, efficiency, radiators, fuel supply, recoverable helium and competing solar/accretion power.

The models remain exploratory. Their outputs must retain model flags and must not be described as validated designs.

## Reproducing the saved screens

The calculations used Python 3.10 with pynucastro 3.1.0, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.15.3, matplotlib 3.10.9 and Numba 0.61.2. In an environment with these dependencies, run `cycle_ledger.py`, `collector_screen.py`, `plant_budget.py`, then `validate.py`. Each writes its result next to its source. `compression_summary.py` can read the compact archived traces without full local simulation output.

Full convergent-flow traces can be regenerated with `compression_probe.py`; parameters, resolution and tolerances are recorded in each JSON result. For example, `python compression_probe.py --stage B1 --zones 128` reruns that branch. `archive_traces.py` retains the time series needed by the downstream radiation and density screens, together with hashes of the full local traces. Full intermediate isotope histories are omitted from the repository to avoid unnecessary binary size.

The nonneutral branch has a separate reproducible chain: `read_padf.py`, `nonneutral_screen.py`, `validate_nonneutral.py`, then `plot_nonneutral.py`. It reads the archived PADF-2 evaluation. Its positive energy-transfer cases precede angular-recycling and other plant costs; the specified 95% beam-recycling cases fail after angular acceptance is charged. See `NONNEUTRAL-BEAMS.md` before interpreting any field-mass-only result.

The thermal-ion branch uses `thermal_neutron_screen.py`, `penning_equilibrium.py`, `scan_penning.py`, `thermal_penning_sensitivity.py`, `thermal_recovery_sensitivity.py`, `validate_thermal_penning.py` and `plot_thermal_penning.py`. Read [THERMAL-NONNEUTRAL.md](THERMAL-NONNEUTRAL.md) for the process, confinement and recovery assumptions before interpreting its output.

The broad catalyst branch is reproduced with `catalyst_catalog.py`, `catalyst_thermal.py`, `catalyst_penning.py --target 49Ti` (repeat for `51V`, `53Cr`, `55Mn`), `catalyst_sensitivity.py`, `validate_catalysts.py`, then `plot_catalysts.py`. Its original PADF archive and compressed parsed data are included. See [CATALYST-COMPARISON.md](CATALYST-COMPARISON.md) before interpreting the field-only output.

The shared-solenoid branch uses `optimize_titanium_source.py`, `bundle_solenoid.py`, `scan_bundle.py`, `bundle_sensitivity.py`, `ion_radiation_and_ends.py`, `end_transfer_sensitivity.py`, `validate_bundle.py` and `plot_bundle.py`. The 675-case scan is archived in deterministic gzip form. Read [SHARED-SOLENOID.md](SHARED-SOLENOID.md) before interpreting its full-mass allocations.
