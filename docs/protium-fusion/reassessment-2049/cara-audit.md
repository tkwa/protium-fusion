The present calculation establishes a conditional budget, while the proposed history must supply the successful apparatus and its measured integrated burden. The final conservative plant ledger closes at **272.369 GW net, 1.3399976×10^15 kg, and 0.203261 mW/kg**. Its additional processing consumes **753.434 GW**. It retains fixed original auxiliaries, full central solar luminosity at 80 AU, 60% heat conversion, 50 K radiators, and the extra 10^14 kg / 100 GW integration reserve. ## The RF envelope and its limits

The 60% heat-conversion source has B=137.149 T, Ti bore radius 22.616 mm and primary rate 1.31859×10^11 per metre per second. At E=1 MV/m, the ideal initially resting particle reaches 60 MeV after **26.120 ns of retarded pulse time**, **1.04845 μs of particle flight**, and **306.486 m**. The carrier is 3.839 THz. These two times have different physical meanings; the calculation correctly avoids charging every location for the full particle flight time.

The uniform-bore wave has 4.265 MW peak power and 0.111415 J unloaded pulse energy. First-order synchrotron emission along the ideal orbit is 0.272202 MeV. The selected parametric schedule uses two directional pulses per attempt, 1 km cells, power attenuation 0.01/m, 50% RF generation efficiency, 0.000906 prompt pairs per primary, and a diagnostic 100 MeV/s heating/radiation burden per trapped positron. It assigns 120 MeV of RF work per eventual removal, including failed kicks and acceleration radiation. That last figure is an explicit allowance.

All processors require access. Their equal-field/equal-duty guide volume is **1.3588695 times** the Ti-only normalization. This audit identified that correction; the final screen includes it. For independent removal probability p=0.1 per complete attempt, the corrected optimum is 1.11153 attempts/s and 8.54676 s mean residence. The cost is **0.918418 MeV/primary**: 0.350489 empty-guide electricity, 0.350489 accumulated positron burden, and 0.217440 particle RF work. Stage-specific source distributions and schedules can change this result.

The population expression is exact for constant births and independent repeated removal: the mean residence is `(1/p−1/2)/f`. It cannot conceal a permanently inaccessible subpopulation. A separate tail-removal mechanism must have a measured residual fraction and cost. The saved rare-reset example uses the old 70% source and is correctly quarantined from the selected 60% plant.

End feeding alone transmits only exp(−10) of its power through 1 km. Distributed feeds must replace guide dissipation and particle beam loading. The two initially empty pulses carry only 0.223 J, while the effective particle work exceeds 2 J per Ti-equivalent cell and attempt. This is consistently charged as an additional energy source; it cannot be delivered by the unloaded input pulse alone. Finite source/coupler/switch peak ratings, pulse storage and sharing remain hardware design requirements. Small average duty does not by itself establish their mass.

## Why the simple pulse cannot establish capture

For a luminous traveling wave in uniform longitudinal B, without other forces, `s=γ−u_z` is invariant. Resonance requires `ωs=Ω`. A constant-amplitude detuned pulse can add at most `2a/|δ|` transverse normalized momentum, where `a=eE/(mc)` and `δ=Ω/s−ω`. Reaching the selected momentum requires a fractional detuning halfwidth below **3.174×10^-6**. This generous maximum-amplitude bound already defeats a simple 10% warm-population capture assumption.

For illustrative axial Maxwellian temperatures of 100 eV, 1 keV and 10 keV, even allowing two separate optimally placed resonance bands gives fractions only 3.62×10^-4, 1.15×10^-4 and 3.62×10^-5. Substitution into the otherwise unchanged optimized screen gives 12.17, 21.47 and 38.01 MeV/primary. These are illustrative distributions, not predictions of the actual trapped positron state. Assuming fresh independent sampling of those bands is itself favorable. Repeated identical pulses need not encounter fresh susceptible particles.

Standing or transverse fields, longitudinal shaping, guide dispersion, boundaries and magnetic taper can break the simple invariant and create genuine phase trapping or prepare a narrow beam. The monochromatic bound therefore does not prohibit the proposed replacement. Simply labeling the original pulse “chirped” would not supply the missing phase-space transport or its power cost. The chosen history should identify the separate capture stage and measured integrated performance.

Published accelerator evidence supports prepared beams. [LaPointe et al. (1996)](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.76.2718) demonstrated high RF-to-beam transfer with an injected electron beam. [Hafizi, Sprangle and Hirshfield (1993)](https://proceedings.jacow.org/p93/PDF/PAC1993_2584.PDF) treated waveguide dispersion, beam loading and axial stalling; [Wang and Hirshfield (1998)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.57.7184) analyzed multistage acceleration. These establish relevant mechanisms and design constraints. Their results do not measure capture of a diffuse warm positron population inside this ion source, or the wall-plug efficiency of its terahertz hardware.

## Requirements the selected history must actually resolve

Small radiated energy does not guarantee phase lock. Radiation reaction, waveguide phase velocity, the source radial field, source rotation, collisions, finite end fields and mode competition all perturb a resonance whose simple tolerance is only a few parts per million. The initially resting ideal orbit is a useful scale calculation; a tapered prepared-beam design requires a self-consistent phase and field solution.

The ion reviewer confirms that the selected layout uses physical 1 km ion cells with local collector/barrier regions. Subdividing the saved 20.24 km active column adds barriers, fringe regions, RF access, support and transfer equipment. Maintaining the same fractional Maxwellian proton end loss at kT=1.6 MeV raises the illustrative barrier by about 4.8 MV through `(kT/e) ln(L_old/L_new)`. The 60 MeV exit target retains nominal headroom, while complete fields and circulation require qualification. Treating 1 km merely as an RF feed interval would leave a longer particle route to the actual collector, during which synchrotron loss and electric fields change the escape margin. No detailed topology is granted by the active-volume factor.

Residual electrons, ionized gas and heavy reaction products remain coupled work. The earlier millisecond electron escape benchmark assumes absorbing boundaries; it does not establish electrostatic collection, secondary suppression or energy recovery. Each ionized hydrogen molecule creates positive charge alongside its electron. Positive-product inventory, rotation and potential handling must be included, and equal initial opposite-charge potential energies must not be mistaken for a free net energy source. The gas source and prompt-pair range are scenarios rather than measured full-production bounds. Photon conversion, uncounted nuclear transitions and surface recirculation can raise the burden.

At 1.75 MeV total incremental processing, the illustrative 0.918418 envelope leaves **0.831582 MeV/primary** for its omitted preparation, tails and other incremental particle handling. This is a budget remainder, not a derived price for those systems. The original extraction, atomic preparation, thermal and coherent circulation charges already remain in the base ledger; the proposed measured total must use that same accounting boundary. The heat-transport screen also uses part of the 10^14 kg / 100 GW reserve, so the entire reserve cannot simultaneously be assigned to RF hardware.

A defensible selected history is consequently a successful integrated station with broad capture, a prepared acceleration stage, qualified collectors and product recovery, and measured performance near 1.75 MeV/primary inside the retained equipment allowances. Its 0.20 mW/kg result follows from that stipulated future engineering performance and the audited ledger. Present solver incompleteness gives no basis for insisting that such a civilization must fail by 2049; the conditional RF arithmetic alone gives no basis for claiming the future apparatus has already been designed.
