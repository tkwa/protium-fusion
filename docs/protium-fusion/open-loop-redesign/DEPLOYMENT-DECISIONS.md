# Revised Moravec deployment decisions ✨

Thomas requested an external-carbon opaque redesign and the best supported net useful power per installed kilogram, with a 0.007 fresh C:H supply budget. This selection applies that request to the already authorized late-2040s article history. It supersedes the Moravec local-factory choice in `reassessment-2049/`; the earlier packet and manifest remain unchanged. The Oriel history, September 2049 commissioning and 0.20 mW/kg account are retained.

## Selected in-universe outcome

The enclosed Moravec station opens in November 2049 at 5 AU, using supplied carbon and extensive catalyst and receiver-material reuse. Its nominal operating point is `operating-cases.json` → `high_source_rating_surface`: radius 1.1×10⁹ m, mass 1.2152×10²³ kg, net output 3.3610×10²⁰ W and net specific power 2.7658 mW/kg. Article figures round to 1.2×10²³ kg, 3.4×10²⁰ W and 2.8 mW/kg. The original open range remains at 120 AU. Construction begins in 2043–2045, with Venus structural stock and a substantial outer-planet carbon-recovery programme.

The station purchases 0.007 kg fresh carbon per kg fresh hydrogen. Fuel makeup uses 0.0056, and 0.0014 covers net carbon loss from receiving and other equipment. The coupled reference returns 99.96276% of CNO and hydrogen per pass. Gross refurbished carbon remains inside the station or is recovered for reuse; it does not all become imported makeup. The selected supply rate is approximately 2.53×10⁶ kg hydrogen and 1.77×10⁴ kg total carbon per second.

N15 pulses recur on average every 113 minutes, N14 every 7.5 days, C12 every 13 days and C13 roughly twice per year. Large pulses have at least a one-day separation. One-day cold-flow buffers and one full batch of each stage are counted within working inventory and the installed reserve. Slow material is collected or protected between major incoming/outgoing flows and returned for processing. Warm incoming fuel receives protection or controlled staging before the final hot convergence.

The selected hardware qualifies the source's complete 100 W/kg peak rating, 60% preparation efficiency, 40% heat conversion, 99% energy capture, 5,000 kg/m² carbon absorber and 1,000 kg/m² processing/structural/thermal service allowance. The nominal 1,000 K cooled wall and 450 K radiator choices support the finite repeated-pulse temperature bound near 1,965 K. Twenty percent installed reserve and recurring replacement at 10⁻⁴ of mass per year, costing 100 MJ/kg, are included.

## Evidence boundary

These hardware qualifications, integrated collection performance and commissioning events are invented future history chosen under Thomas's instruction. The article states them in universe. The research calculations establish the required balances and identify physical qualifications; they do not experimentally demonstrate those devices or predict an actual 2049 deployment.

The completed B2 result resolves the artificial-viscosity failure. Its remaining spatial sensitivity is recorded, and the coherent all-stage 64-zone family supplies the selected account. Replacing B2 alone with its matched 256-zone refinement raises the process electrical margin by about 0.82%, while other stages remain at 64 zones. This small whole-cycle change does not remove uncertainty in preparation or collection.

In particular, electromagnetic slowing must reduce the integrated nuclear interaction probability before fuel reaches carbon. There is no energy below which every possible exothermic reaction is forbidden. The material allowance requires mean irreversible target-carbon loss below roughly 6.2×10⁻⁵ atoms per incident fuel nucleus if the receiver consumes the entire structural allowance. The selected history assumes successful qualification of braking, fast neutral tails, implantation, substrate separation and repeated carbon reuse. Their complete coupled transport calculation remains outstanding in real research.

The finite search's 3.49 mW/kg edge has essentially no allowance for previous-shot heating, and is not selected. The 10.5 mW/kg finite-depth example depends on an uncalculated escaping photon spectrum and is also not selected. The article uses the rounded surface-heating case with its separate finite-slab check. It makes no claim to a global optimum or mature future fleet performance.

## Economic changes

At 100 MJ/kg, construction energy takes about 1,146 years to repay from the selected net output. Giving all installed material a 30 km/s cruise speed requires 5.47×10³¹ J of kinetic energy; extraction, propulsion inefficiency, Solar orbital-energy changes and receiving works add to that. The comparison of 30% solar conversion and 1 kg/m² complete collector mass reaches equal W/kg near 384 AU. External feed is the station boundary condition; upstream extraction and delivery apparatus remain a separate account. These changes support an early fuel-cycle and long-duration-power programme while preserving the large capital burden.
