# Working source index

Sources listed here are leads and inputs, not an endorsement of every claim. Separate published measurement, published calculation and this investigation's extrapolations.

## Nuclear physics and plasma models

- pynucastro 3.1.0: <https://pynucastro.github.io/pynucastro/>. Installed in an isolated temporary Python directory. The included ReacLib rate records must be archived with final calculations.
- Beta-limited cycle example: <https://pynucastro.github.io/pynucastro/beta-limited-rates.html>.
- EOS implementation and limitations: <https://pynucastro.github.io/pynucastro/stellar-eos.html>. Ideal ions, radiation, arbitrary-relativity/degeneracy electrons and positrons; no Coulomb corrections.
- Denissenkov et al. (2013), nova models: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.5209>.
- Fujibayashi et al. (2024), hot-CNO energy and neutrino accounting: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11572>.
- Gyurky et al. (2019), 14N(p,gamma)15O resonance strengths: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.07446>. Relevant stopping inputs are estimates used in the experiment, not all directly measured by this paper.

- Ochs et al. (2026), bremsstrahlung trapping in p–11B inertial fusion: <https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0305034>. A different nuclear fuel; use radiation-transport reasoning, not its fuel-specific thresholds, for CNO.
- Rider (1997), nonequilibrium fusion limitations: <https://doi.org/10.1063/1.872556>. Read its stated plasma assumptions before applying its results.
- Whitmire (1975), catalytic nuclear ramjets: <https://doi.org/10.1016/0094-5765(75)90063-6>. Historical conceptual proposal; full engineering feasibility remains to be checked.

## Collector and driver context

- NIST carbon photon attenuation and energy absorption: <https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/XrayMassCoef/ElemTab/z06.html>. At 1 MeV, mu/rho=0.06361 cm2/g and mu_en/rho=0.02792 cm2/g. These differ; an exponential using mu_en does not replace thick-wall scattering transport.
- Mitteau, Diez and Firdaouss (2024), tungsten surface-transient design rule: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13269>, full text <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.13269>. Motivates heat-flux-factor/fatigue checks; one illustrative calculation uses k=130 W/m/K and cp=140 J/kg/K. Its printed density appears to have an extra zero; our screen uses 19300 kg/m3, consistent with [NIST material constants](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/XrayMassCoef/tab1.html). Its surface-loading cycle-life rule does not apply to volumetric gamma heating or carbon.
- ITER divertor: <https://www.iter.org/machine/divertor>. Context for designed steady/transient heat fluxes, not validation of this collector or particle damage.
- HYLIFE-II author manuscript: <https://www.ralphmoir.com/media/hylifeII.pdf>. Liquid-wall inertial-fusion capture lead; detailed comparison remains to be done.
- Pulsed-liner report: <https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/publications/PDF/csp_008c/pdf/icp_10.pdf>. Electrical-to-liner kinetic efficiency does not establish electrical-to-CNO-fuel efficiency.
- Heavy-ion driver review: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06508>. Accelerator efficiency and transport motivate investigation, without establishing this giant convergent flow.
